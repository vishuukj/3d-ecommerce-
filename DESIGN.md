## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium, dark luxury minimalist aesthetic engineered for high-fidelity spatial audio and master-crafted acoustic hardware. It targets audiophiles, industrial design connoisseurs, and affluent creators who demand uncompromising acoustic precision paired with sculptural industrial elegance.

The visual mood evokes an acoustic anechoic chamber fused with a private luxury gallery: obsidian depths, brushed titanium highlights, champagne bronze warm-resonance accents, and laser-precise spatial cyan illumination. The interface feels weightless yet grounded, technical yet sensual, and engineered to microscopic tolerances.

### Design Movement & Ethos
- **Dark Luxury Minimalist + Precision Glassmorphism:** Deep obsidian base planes layered with micro-frosted translucent dark panels (`backdrop-blur-md`), whisper-thin metallic ghost borders (0.5px to 1px), and low-diffusion directional glows.
- **Architectural Typography:** Clinical, modern geometric sans paired with mono-spaced scientific readout styling to elevate raw hardware telemetry (frequency responses, transducer metallurgy, decoding bitrates) into structural art.
- **Tactile Kinetic Feedback:** Fluid, dampened physical micro-interactions mirroring weighted volume dials, precision-machined knurled knobs, and magnetic latching.

## Colors

The palette establishes an absolute high-end atmospheric hierarchy centered on void-black acoustics, warm metallic luxury, and cold spatial frequencies.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#D4AF37` / Acoustic Champagne Bronze):** The core signifier of luxury craftsmanship, warm acoustic resonance, and master-grade hardware. Reserved for hero actions, verified audiophile badges, bespoke material badges, and high-value conversion points.
- **Secondary (`#00F0FF` / Spatial Neon Cyan):** Symbolizes spatial audio dispersion, dimensional stage tracking, beamforming arrays, and active interactive state signals. Used sparingly for interactive focal points, active wave nodes, and 3D exploded view triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#E2E8F0` / Metallic Titanium Silver):** Cool brushed aerospace metal tone. Drives prominent headline typography, structural framing, slider tracks, and pristine metallic micro-icons.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#0A0B0E` / Deep Obsidian):** Deep non-pure black that absorbs visual noise, preventing eye fatigue while granting supreme contrast to hardware lighting and luminous 3D renders.

### Functional & Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** `#0A0B0E` (Deep Obsidian)
- **Elevated Surface (Stealth Charcoal):** `#14161C` (Layer 1 containers and side sheets)
- **Translucent Plate:** `rgba(20, 22, 28, 0.65)` with `12px` backdrop blur
- **Subtle Surface:** `#1C1F26` (Input backgrounds and inactive track toggles)
- **Hairline Structural Borders:** `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.12)` on resting elements; `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)` on active or focused states.
- **Muted Scientific Text:** `#94A3B8` (Slate secondary readouts and spec descriptors)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances architectural tension with clinical industrial precision:

1. **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Clean, geometric, and authoritative. Tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) creates a machined, engineered feel in titanium silver shades.
2. **Editorial & Body (`Inter`):** Neutral, razor-sharp legibility designed for product storytelling, acoustic breakdown narratives, and editorial soundstage descriptions.
3. **Hardware Telemetry & Specs (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to technical metrics (e.g., `40mm BERYLLIUM`, `192kHz / 24-BIT`, `ANC: -38dB`, `LATENCY: 12ms`). Monospaced alignment gives every card, viewport overlay, and data badge an analytical, laboratory-grade authenticity.

### Formatting Rules
- Render all `label-caps` and `spec-code-sm` metrics in uppercase with generous tracking (`0.08em` to `0.12em`).
- Numerical acoustic values (dB, Hz, kHz, Ω) should always be accompanied by their units in the secondary muted color (`#94A3B8`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an open, fluid 12-column grid system calibrated to give physical hardware 3D models and audio components expansive negative space.

### Grid Anatomy
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 fluid columns, `1.25rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Elements stack vertically with fixed floating navigation pills pinned to the bottom thumb zone.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 fluid columns, `2.5rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Split-screen hardware viewer and technical specification panels.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12 fluid columns with a max content width of `1440px`, `4.5rem` margins, `2.5rem` gutters. Allows asymmetric placement: persistent 3D spatial viewport locked to the left/center while configuration swatches and spec sheets glide along the right rail.

### Spacing Philosophy
- Ample macro vertical rhythm (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) isolates flagship product components, reinforcing exclusivity and deliberate curation.
- Micro component pacing (`space-xs` to `space-md`) ensures tightly grouped parameter grids, swatches, and hardware telemetry readouts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows, but on **luminous depth, controlled surface luminescence, and precision edge lighting**.

### Surface Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Anechoic Floor):** Pure `#0A0B0E` canvas. Non-interactive backdrop.
2. **Level 1 (Stealth Charcoal):** `#14161C` solid or `rgba(20, 22, 28, 0.7)` frosted plate with `12px` backdrop blur. Used for product specification cards, filter decks, and modal backings. Outlined with `1px` solid `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.08)`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Interactive Plates):** `#1C1F26` with `1px` subtle top highlight line `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)` simulating an overhead studio light catch on milled aluminum.
4. **Level 3 (Floating HUD Elements):** Floating pill navigation and spatial audio controls hover with ambient occlusion: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.15)`.

### Spatial & Acoustic Illumination
- **Acoustic Warmth Glow:** Hovering primary buttons and selected luxury swatches emit an ultra-soft champagne flare: `0 0 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.
- **Spatial Signal Pulse:** Active spatial audio nodes, 360-degree rotation axes, and frequency visualizer peaks cast a targeted cyan laser bloom: `0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances the soft radius of acoustic resonance chambers with the sharp, machined tolerances of aircraft-grade metals.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to technical spec chips, interactive inputs, parameter tiles, and material selectors.
- **Large Radius (`1rem` / 16px):** Standard for product showcase cards, audio analysis modules, and floating viewport frames.
- **Pill Silhouette (`rounded-full`):** Reserved for primary interactive controls, category switchers, floating navigation bars, and exploded view control anchors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Luxury Action:** Rounded-full pill shape. Filled with Acoustic Champagne Bronze (`#D4AF37`), text in Deep Obsidian (`#0A0B0E`, `font-semibold`). Micro-glow on hover with subtle forward scale `1.02`.
- **Secondary Stealth Action:** Frosted glass background (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.05)`), border `1px` metallic titanium (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.2)`), text in Titanium Silver (`#E2E8F0`). On hover, border transitions to Spatial Cyan (`#00F0FF`).
- **Ghost Spatial Action:** Frameless with monospace technical label and directional icon. Text defaults to Slate (`#94A3B8`) shifting to Cyan with a line sweep animation.

### Floating Pill Navigation
- Centered at bottom on mobile or floating top center on desktop.
- Translucent dark backdrop (`rgba(10, 11, 14, 0.8)` with `16px` blur) encased in a hairline `1px` titanium border.
- Integrated mini-frequency visualizer pulse reflecting active platform audio state.

### Material Swatch Chips
- Round or soft squircle selectors displaying tactile textures: **Forged Carbon** (geometric dark weave), **Anodized Titanium** (radial brushed metallic sheen), **Saddle Leather** (warm rich amber-brown grain), and **Matte Ceramic** (smooth chalk-black).
- Active state indicated by an external concentric ring in Champagne Bronze separated by a `2px` void gap.

### 3D Viewport Containers & Exploded View Triggers
- Translucent viewport chamber with an ambient radial dark gradient centered beneath the 3D model.
- **360-Degree Indicator:** Circular hairline compass ring with rotating cyan beacon tracking the user's drag angle.
- **Exploded View Trigger:** Circular micro-button showing driver schematics. When triggered, driver layers separate with step-indexed technical callouts rendered in `spec-code-sm`.

### Acoustic Frequency Visualizers
- Real-time or simulated 24-band spectrum visualizer.
- Vertical audio bars using dual tone gradients transitioning from Titanium Silver at low frequencies to Spatial Cyan at acoustic peaks (10kHz - 40kHz).

### Cards & Technical Data Grids
- **Acoustic Spec Grid:** Modular 2-column or 4-column layout where each spec features a large titanium headline number (`Space Grotesk`) and a monospace uppercase caption (`JetBrains Mono`).
- Hairline divider grid lines (`rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.06)`) without heavy outer card borders to preserve industrial lightness.
